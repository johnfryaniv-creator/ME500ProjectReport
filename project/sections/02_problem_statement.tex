\section{Problem statement}

We seek the solution of \cref{eq:pde} on $0 \le x \le L$ subject to
\begin{align}
  u(0,t) &= U_0 \cos(\omega t), \label{eq:bc0}\\
  u(L,t) &= 0. \label{eq:bcL}
\end{align}
The left end is driven harmonically at frequency $\omega$ with amplitude
$U_0$, and the right end is fixed. Because the rod is damped ($\eta>0$), any
transient that depends on the initial conditions decays over time. What
remains is the \emph{steady-state} response, which oscillates at the driving
frequency $\omega$. This steady state is what we derive.
